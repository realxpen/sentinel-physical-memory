# Business

The hackathon wedge is a facility / operations manager responsible for a small-to-medium commercial space, demonstrated with one office.

Longer-term markets may include property management, factories, schools, clinics/hospitals, hotels, retail, construction and homes, but the build must not broaden into those vertical-specific products before the core environmental-memory loop is proven.

Future business work should document: buyer pain, frequency of observation, ROI from faster issue/change awareness, deployment model, data/privacy requirements, and path from office wedge to adjacent environments.
