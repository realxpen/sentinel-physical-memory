# DEC-002 — Hackathon MVP Scope

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: Accepted

## Decision

Build one complete controlled-office loop for one facility / operations manager.

The required path is:

`Scan A → remember → ask → physical change → Scan B → diff → prioritize → plan → fix → Scan C → verify`

## In scope

- short phone walkthrough
- selected-frame multimodal perception
- structured evidence-backed environmental state
- durable environmental memory/history
- contextual Ask
- Reality Diff
- lightweight action plan
- verification
- real Nebius/NVIDIA runtime inference

## Out of scope

- marketplace/provider hiring
- payments
- robotics/IoT control
- autonomous repair
- full BIM/metric 3D reconstruction
- enterprise multi-tenancy
- complex auth
- full facility-management suite
- unnecessary integrations

## Rationale

The broader concept is a startup roadmap. The hackathon must make the single novel claim undeniable and reproducible in under three minutes.

## Revisit condition

Only expand scope after the complete closed loop is reliable on the controlled office demo and Phase 15 reliability checks are passing.
